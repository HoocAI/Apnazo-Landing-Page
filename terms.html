Apnazo Merchant & Partner Terms of Service

Version 1.2 · Effective Date: November 27, 2026

1. Commercial Operations & Commission

Apnazo operates a local hyper-delivery and super app platform across Rourkela, Odisha. Platform commission rates are capped between 5% and 10% as specified in your category schedule, with a 0% promotional commission during your first 30 days of active onboarding.

2. Payout Settlements (T+2 Days)

All net order earnings are credited directly to the merchant's verified bank account on a T+2 business day rolling settlement schedule. Zero setup fees or recurring software maintenance charges apply.

3. Product Quality, Returns & Consumer Protection

Merchants guarantee that all food, grocery, and packaged products delivered are fresh, unexpired, and accurately represented. A standard 24-hour return and replacement window applies to customer orders.

4. Statutory Licences & 15-Day Grace Period

Merchants operating in food and grocery categories must maintain an active FSSAI licence. If a licence expires, Apnazo provides an official 15-day grace period during which sales continuity is maintained while renewal documents are processed.

5. Dispute Resolution

Commercial or order fulfillment disputes are reviewed by the Apnazo Partner Desk within 24 hours. Contact our helpline at +91 7355315892.
